You Don't Need to Be an Expert to Start.

At Econovo, we understand that entering the world of business and fintech can feel overwhelming. That's why our core value is Growth. Whether you're deeply passionate about finance, or just curious about entrepreneurship, you have a safe space here to learn, fail, and build.

What We Do

Our Core Focus Areas

Through workshops, projects, and discussions, we help you master these four pillars.

Economics

Learn beyond the classroom. We dissect how global forces and local markets actually work.

Business

Acquire real management skills. We analyze case studies and build actionable strategies.

Innovation

Transform creative ideas into practical projects. Entrepreneurship is about execution.

FinTech

Explore the future of finance. Understand block-chain, digital banking, and modern tech.

How It Works

Your Journey With Us

A clear path from a curious student to a confident problem solver.

1. Curiosity & Onboarding

Attend our welcoming sessions, meet the team, and discover which fields ignite your passion without any pressure.

2. Skill Building Workshops

Participate in hands-on workshops led by seniors and experts. No boring lectures, only interactive learning.

3. Collaborative Projects

Work in teams to solve real problems. Apply what you've learned in a safe, supportive environment.

4. Becoming a Problem Solver

Graduate from a learner to a doer. Build your portfolio, expand your network, and prepare for your career.
